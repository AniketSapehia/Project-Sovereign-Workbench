The highest vibration reading is 6.4 mm/s RMS, which falls within Zone C. According to SOP-MECH-004, the corrective actions required are:
1. Common causes of high vibration are unbalance, misalignment, bearing wear, looseness, cavitation and pipe strain. The spectrum analysis report must identify the most likely cause before parts are ordered.
2. Bearing replacement must be done together with a laser alignment check of the pump-motor coupling.
3. After any corrective work the pump must return to Zone A or Zone B.

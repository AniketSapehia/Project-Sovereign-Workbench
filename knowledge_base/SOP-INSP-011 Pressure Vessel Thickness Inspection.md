# SOP-INSP-011: Ultrasonic Thickness Inspection of Pressure Vessels

SAMPLE DOCUMENT — fictional "Sample Refinery Ltd." — created for demonstrating the Sovereign Workbench prototype only. Values are illustrative and must not be used for real equipment.

Revision: 2 | Effective date: 15-06-2025 | Owner: Inspection Department

## 1. Purpose

To define the method for ultrasonic thickness (UT) measurement of pressure vessel shells and heads, the calculation of corrosion rate and remaining life, and the fixing of the next inspection date.

## 2. Measurement Method

Thickness is measured with a calibrated digital UT gauge at the fixed Condition Monitoring Locations (CMLs) marked on the vessel inspection drawing. At each CML, three readings are taken and the LOWEST value is recorded. The gauge calibration must be verified on a reference block before and after the survey.

## 3. Key Definitions

Original thickness (t_original): the nominal thickness at the time of construction, from the vessel data sheet.
Minimum required thickness (t_min): the thickness needed to safely hold the design pressure, from the vessel data sheet or design calculation.
Actual thickness (t_actual): the lowest thickness measured in the current survey.

## 4. Corrosion Rate Calculation

Long-term corrosion rate (mm/year) = (t_original − t_actual) ÷ years in service.

If a previous survey is available, the short-term corrosion rate = (t_previous − t_actual) ÷ years between surveys. The HIGHER of the long-term and short-term rates must be used.

## 5. Remaining Life Calculation

Remaining life (years) = (t_actual − t_min) ÷ corrosion rate.

## 6. Next Inspection Date

The next internal or UT inspection must be carried out within HALF of the remaining life, or 10 years, whichever is LESS.

Example: if remaining life is 6 years, the next inspection is due within 3 years.

## 7. Acceptance and Escalation

If t_actual is greater than t_min, the vessel is fit for continued service until the next inspection date.
If remaining life is less than 4 years, the Inspection Engineer must raise an approval note for repair or replacement planning in the next turnaround.
If t_actual is equal to or less than t_min, the vessel must be taken out of service and a fitness-for-service assessment carried out before restart.

## 8. Records

The UT survey sheet, calculations and the next inspection date are entered in the equipment history file. The Inspection Engineer signs the survey sheet; the Head of Inspection approves the next inspection date.

# REF-ENG-001: Units and Conversion Factors

SAMPLE DOCUMENT — fictional "Sample Refinery Ltd." reference data sheet, created for demonstrating the Sovereign Workbench prototype. The conversion factors themselves are standard values.

Revision: 1 | Effective date: 01-11-2026 | Owner: Technical Services

## 1. Purpose

Standard conversion factors to be used in all engineering calculations, reports and software, so that every department uses the same values.

## 2. Pressure

1 bar = 100 kPa = 14.5038 psi = 1.01972 kg/cm²
1 psi = 0.0689476 bar = 6.89476 kPa
To convert psi to bar: bar = psi × 0.0689476 (or psi ÷ 14.5038). Check: 14.5038 psi = 1 bar; 100 psi = 6.89476 bar.
To convert bar to psi: psi = bar × 14.5038. Check: 1 bar = 14.5038 psi.
1 kg/cm² = 0.980665 bar = 14.2233 psi
1 MPa = 10 bar = 145.038 psi
1 atm = 1.01325 bar = 14.6959 psi
Gauge and absolute pressure: bar(a) = bar(g) + 1.01325 at standard atmosphere.

## 3. Temperature

°F = °C × 9/5 + 32. Check: 100 °C = 212 °F.
°C = (°F − 32) × 5/9. Check: 32 °F = 0 °C.
K = °C + 273.15

## 4. Flow and volume

1 m³/h = 4.40287 US gpm (US gallons per minute)
1 US gallon = 3.78541 litres
1 barrel (oil, bbl) = 158.987 litres = 42 US gallons
1 bbl/day = 0.00662447 m³/h. Check: 1000 bbl/day = 6.62447 m³/h.

## 5. Length and mass

1 inch = 25.4 mm. 1 foot = 0.3048 m.
1 kg = 2.20462 lb. 1 lb = 0.453592 kg.

## 6. Power and energy

1 kW = 1.34102 hp. 1 hp = 0.745700 kW.
1 kcal = 4.1868 kJ. 1 BTU = 1.05506 kJ.

## 7. Vibration velocity

1 in/s = 25.4 mm/s. Vibration limits in SOP-MECH-004 are in mm/s RMS.

# SOP-ADM-002: Approval Notes and Delegation of Financial Powers

## 1. Purpose

To standardise the format of internal approval notes and to state who may approve expenditure of different values.

## 2. Standard Format of an Approval Note

Every approval note must contain the following sections, in this order:

1. Subject: one line stating what approval is sought.
2. Reference: inspection report numbers, earlier correspondence, SOP clauses.
3. Background: the situation in brief, with equipment tag numbers.
4. Findings: the key facts and measured values that justify the proposal.
5. Proposal: exactly what is proposed to be done, and by when.
6. Financial implication: estimated cost (in ₹), budget head, and whether it is within budget.
7. Approval sought: the specific approval, addressed to the competent authority as per Section 3.

The note is signed by the initiating officer and recommended by the Department Head before it goes to the approving authority.

## 3. Delegation of Financial Powers (Revenue Expenditure, Maintenance)

Up to ₹5,00,000 (5 lakh): Department Head.
Above ₹5 lakh and up to ₹25,00,000 (25 lakh): General Manager of the department.
Above ₹25 lakh and up to ₹1,00,00,000 (1 crore): Executive Director (Operations).
Above ₹1 crore: Director (Refineries).

Emergency work required to protect safety of people or plant may be started with the approval of the General Manager regardless of value, with post-facto approval of the competent authority within 7 days.

## 4. Language and Style

Approval notes should be brief, factual and written in the third person. Every measured value must state its unit and source document. Opinions must be clearly separated from facts.
